# Management memo — September 28, 2026 information cutoff

**To:** CFO, VP Finance, Head of Finance & Strategy<br>
**Subject:** Which drivers turn scale into operating profit?

## Executive conclusion

**RECOMMENDATION:** Focus on protecting trading engagement and realized monetization, converting net deposits into earning balances and deeper relationships, and gating technology, marketing, and G&A spend against realized operating-profit conversion. Treat trading demand and interest rates as scenario variables, not committed growth sources. This is an independent public-data analysis, not Robinhood guidance.

## What the historical data shows

**FACT:** Revenue rose from $2.95b in 2024 to $4.47b in 2025; funded customers and assets also expanded. **DERIVED:** Measured activity contributed $621m of $862m in the exact 2024 versus 2023 transaction-revenue bridge. Acquisitions and product launches make later bridges less directly comparable. Gold subscription was only 4.0% of 2025 revenue. [Historical analysis](HISTORICAL_ANALYSIS.md); [2025 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1783879/000178387926000029/hood-20251231.htm).

## What the forecast requires

**MODEL RESULT:** After the interest audit, Base reaches $5.87b of 2027 revenue and 43.5% GAAP operating margin; 2028 reaches $6.33b and 43.9%. The 2027 scenario revenue range is $4.41b–$6.74b. Base requires roughly 0.3m net customers and $17.7b net deposits per normal quarter, sustained product activity, and operating expenses growing more slowly than revenue. 2026 contains two actual and two forecast quarters; Q3 is a nowcast, not a reported financial result. [Forecast results](FORECAST_RESULTS.md); [margin audit](OPERATING_MARGIN_AUDIT.md).

## Largest financial sensitivities

**MODEL RESULT:** A 20% broad transaction-monetization decline removes $613m of 2027 revenue. A 20% options-activity decline cuts operating income by $264m. A 100 bp policy-rate decline with explicit analyst pass-through cuts revenue by $265m; Cash Sweep does not receive that policy shock. The separate $899m all-yield stress is wider than company rate exposure. [Sensitivities](SENSITIVITY_ANALYSIS.md); [rate audit](INTEREST_SENSITIVITY_AUDIT.md).

## Strategic priorities

1. Protect options engagement and watch realized revenue per contract and crypto bps. The activity-led historical bridge and transaction-pricing sensitivity make these earnings-critical, but market demand is not controllable.
2. Convert deposits into earning balances and Gold relationships while protecting net spread and credit quality. Balance growth offset weaker yield historically; Gold is growing but remains a modest disclosed share.
3. Gate product and growth spending to realized revenue. The modeled 2027 incremental operating margin is 49.0%, below 2025's 68.3% historical level, and credit provision increases materially. [Decision framework](STRATEGIC_DECISION.md).

## Key risks and KPIs

The two risks are trading/crypto normalization and lower realized yields combined with rising credit or fixed costs. Review funded-customer additions; deposits and earning-balance capture; options contracts/customer and revenue/contract; blended crypto bps; and net realized yield alongside expense/revenue by category each quarter. Internal cohort and product-margin data are needed before converting these priorities into capital commitments. [CFO challenge](CFO_CHALLENGE.md).
